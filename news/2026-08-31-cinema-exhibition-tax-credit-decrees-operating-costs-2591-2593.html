Cinema exhibition tax credit – Decrees on operating costs and investments (rep. 2591 and 2593)

August 31, 2026 · DG Cinema e Audiovisivo

The directorial decrees of 31 August 2026 rep. 2591 (tax credit for cinema operating costs) and rep. 2593 (tax credit for investments in cinemas) publish the outcome of the eligibility assessments of the tax credit claims relating to operating costs and to investments in cinemas. The publication constitutes notice of recognition of the tax credit, with no individual notices sent by certified email (PEC). The credit may be used from the 10th day of the month following the notice of recognition, pursuant to Art. 3 of the 'Ministerial Decree on other tax credits 2021' as amended. The decrees do not include claims already recognised previously, nor those whose assessment is still being finalised.
