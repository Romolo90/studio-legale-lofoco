Distribution tax credit 2025 – Second session, pre-filing until 1 December and final submission on 10 and 11 December (Directorial Decree rep. 3602)

November 23, 2026 · DG Cinema e Audiovisivo

By Directorial Decree of 23 November 2026 rep. 3602 the DG Cinema and Audiovisual opens a second 2025 session of the tax credit for national (TCDN) and international (TCDIF) distribution. The decree notes that in the first session (Directorial Decree of 25 September 2026 rep. 2828) eligible applications remained below the available resources, and makes the unused 2025 resources of Ministerial Decree of 6 March 2025 rep. 55 available for works whose national theatrical distribution or international release took place by 31 December 2025 (Art. 1(2)). Applications are pre-filled on DGCOL from 12:00 on 25 November to 23:59 on 1 December 2026 and submitted in final form from 12:00 on 10 December to 23:59 on 11 December 2026. Only applications pre-filled in the first phase, which gives no priority, can access final submission, and the date and time of the certified e-mail (PEC) generated by DGCOL are decisive (Art. 1(4)-(7)). Applications are examined and granted in the chronological order of final submission and within the available resources, and once the limit is reached the system is disabled (Art. 2(2) and (3)). Distribution costs may not exceed 30% of the total production cost of the work as attested by the producer. Promotion, advertising and marketing costs are eligible even when incurred with parties lacking the requirements of Art. 4(2) of Interministerial Decree of 4 November 2025 rep. 412, provided they are strictly related to the national distribution of the work, and the decree mentions among them campaigns on digital platforms, social media and search engines and the related measurement services (Art. 3). On pain of inadmissibility, applications must include the insurance policy against damage from natural disasters and catastrophic events (Art. 4) and, for applications admitted to examination, the receipt of payment of the examination fee within the mandatory ten-day deadline from the DG's notice (Art. 2(4)).
