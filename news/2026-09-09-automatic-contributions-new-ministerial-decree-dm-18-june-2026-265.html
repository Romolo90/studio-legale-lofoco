Automatic contributions – New ministerial decree (D.M. 18 June 2026 rep. 265)

September 9, 2026 · DG Cinema e Audiovisivo

Ministerial Decree of 18 June 2026 rep. 265 has been published, laying down the implementing rules for automatic contributions under Articles 23, 24 and 25 of Law 220/2016; it repeals and replaces Ministerial Decree of 15 July 2021 rep. 251. The new decree applies to applications for the 2026 funding year (results achieved in 2025); for the 2022–2025 funding years Ministerial Decree 251/2021 continues to apply, together with certain provisions of the new decree, including the obligation to document results on pain of inadmissibility and forfeiture of the benefit if the work does not obtain final Italian nationality. Notable provisions include fully paid-up share capital and net equity of at least EUR 40,000 (EUR 10,000 for short films), and use of the amounts in the company's potential fund by 31 December of the fifth year after they are credited, on pain of forfeiture. Within thirty days of publication the Directorate will adopt the implementing directorial decrees and publish the new application forms.
