Lazio Region – «Lazio Next Generation 2026», EUR 3 million for low-budget works, applications from 15 October to 4 November

October 8, 2026 · Regione Lazio

By determination no. G13701 of 7 October 2026, published together with the call (Annex A) in the Lazio Official Bulletin (BURL) no. 81 of 8 October, the Lazio Region makes EUR 3 million in non-repayable grants available for fiction, documentaries, animation and short films with a total production cost of up to EUR 2.5 million, not yet completed (Art. 5). Applicants must be independent producers, whether or not SMEs, holding the exploitation rights at least for Italy and with an active operating office in Lazio, and each single undertaking may file only one application (Art. 4). By the application date the work must already have applied to the Ministry of Culture for tax credit eligibility and for Italian nationality, including provisional nationality (Art. 5(3)), and must meet at least two territorial requirements, one of which among shooting, crew and post-production in Lazio (Art. 6). The grant is 20% of the costs incurred in Lazio plus a 7% flat rate, and 25% for documentaries, animation, short films and first and second works, with bonuses up to a 30% overall cap (Art. 7) and ceilings of EUR 50,000, 200,000 or 500,000 depending on the cost of the work (Art. 9). Total public aid for the work, including the tax credit, may not exceed 50% of the production cost, save for the higher thresholds under Regulation (EU) 651/2014 (Art. 8). Projects are ranked (Arts. 11-15). Applications, digitally signed, are sent by certified email (PEC) to cinemaaudiovisivo@pec.regione.lazio.it from 12:00 noon on 15 October to 5:00 p.m. on 4 November 2026 (Art. 16), and the application forms will be approved by a separate act.
