Italian nationality for video games – Provisional and final decrees of 30 July 2026

July 30, 2026 · DG Cinema e Audiovisivo

Directorial Decree of 30 July 2026 rep. 2464 ('provisional Italian nationality for video games') and Directorial Decree of 30 July 2026 rep. 2463 ('final Italian nationality for video games') have been published. Publication constitutes formal notice that Italian nationality has been recognised and no individual notices will be sent by certified email. The lists are also available under 'Administrative activities – Italian nationality of works' on the institutional website.
