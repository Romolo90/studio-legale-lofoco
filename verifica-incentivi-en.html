Which Italian incentives to look at for your project

Practical and confidential tool for producers, directors and audiovisual professionals. In a few steps it shows which support measures — Tax Credit, selective grants and European funds — are worth looking at for a project like yours, and which requirements to have checked, based on the official 2026 rules of the Direzione Generale Cinema e Audiovisivo.

Important notice: This is an indicative simulator based on publicly available rules from the Direzione Generale Cinema e Audiovisivo (rules verified against the text of the decrees on 29 September 2026). It does not replace professional advice, the cultural/nationality test, or the actual application process. Results are for guidance only. Always verify the latest official notices.

Production tax credit rates follow Interministerial Decree MiC-MEF of 10 July 2024, rep. no. 225, as amended by Interministerial Decree of 22 April 2025, rep. no. 141 and by Interministerial Decree of 6 July 2026, rep. no. 281: 30% for independent producers of film works (art. 13(1)), 20% for non-independent producers and non-European undertakings (art. 13(3)), 20% for television and web works, raised to 30% in the cases listed in art. 17(1), 30% for documentaries and short films, 35% for animation (arts. 21, 28 and 24). The credit cannot exceed €4m per film work and €7m per television or web work, with annual caps per undertaking; with at least 40% of foreign resources €6m and €10m (art. 5). Only short films with an eligible cost of no more than €200,000 are admitted (art. 27). Applications are processed by start date of shooting, or of production work for animation, until resources run out (art. 6-bis). The nationality test and cultural eligibility still have to be assessed case by case. The tool does not calculate exact amounts and does not replace the DGCOL application.
