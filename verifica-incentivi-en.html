Which Italian incentives to look at for your project

Practical and confidential tool for producers, directors and audiovisual professionals. In a few steps it shows which support measures — Tax Credit, selective grants and European funds — are worth looking at for a project like yours, and which requirements to have checked, based on the official 2026 rules of the Direzione Generale Cinema e Audiovisivo.

Important notice: This is an indicative simulator based on publicly available rules from the Direzione Generale Cinema e Audiovisivo (rules verified against the text of the decrees on 12 September 2026). It does not replace professional advice, the cultural/nationality test, or the actual application process. Results are for guidance only. Always verify the latest official notices.

Client-side engine based on official 2026 DG Cinema notices (incl. 29 May D.D. for TC and videogames, selective contributions, investment attraction). Production tax credit rates follow Interministerial Decree MiC-MEF of 10 July 2024, rep. no. 225, as amended by Interministerial Decree of 22 April 2025, rep. no. 141: 40% for independent producers of film works (art. 13(1)), 30% for non-independent producers and non-European undertakings (art. 13(3)), 25% for television and web works, raised to 35% in the cases listed in art. 17(1). The credit cannot exceed €9m per work, or €18m with at least 30% of foreign resources (art. 5). The nationality test and cultural eligibility still have to be assessed case by case.
