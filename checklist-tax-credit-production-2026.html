Tax Credit Production 2026 Application Checklist

Practical tool by Studio Legale Lo Foco (Rome). Does not replace official assessment, cultural eligibility test or DGCOL submission. Always verify the latest DG Cinema notices.

Updated 12 September 2026. Legal basis: art. 15 of Law no. 220 of 14 November 2016 and Interministerial Decree MiC-MEF of 10 July 2024, rep. no. 225, as amended by Interministerial Decree of 22 April 2025, rep. no. 141 (in force since 6 June 2025).
Rates. Film works: 40% of the eligible cost for independent producers (art. 13(1)); for European independent undertakings with high production and financial capacity, 35% on the portion of eligible cost above €5m and up to €10m, and 30% above €10m (art. 13(2)); 30% for non-independent producers and non-European undertakings, up to €5m per year per undertaking or group (art. 13(3)). Television and web works: 25%, raised to 35% in the cases listed in art. 17(1).
Caps. The credit cannot exceed €9m per work, raised to €18m where at least 30% of the total production cost is covered by resources from outside Italy (art. 5).

1. Preliminary requirements

  • Italian nationality (or majority Italian co-production) or cultural eligibility obtained / in progress
  • Subjective requirements: limited company with fully paid-up share capital and net equity of at least €40,000 (€10,000 for short films), registered office in the European Economic Area, taxation in Italy, ATECO code J 59.11 (art. 2(4))
  • For fiction film works longer than 52 minutes: private financing covering at least 40% of the production cost, excluding the requested tax credit, and a binding agreement with a distributor (art. 12(1)); alternatively, works awarded a selective grant or funding from EU or Council of Europe bodies (art. 12(2))
  • For television and web fiction: total running time of at least 52 minutes and total cost of at least €2,000 per minute, save for derogation by the Directorate General (art. 16(6))
  • Preliminary application filed before significant expenditure begins (exceptions apply)
  • Legal representative with SPID/CIE access to DGCOL

2. Documents to prepare (core list)

  • Digitally signed application (DGCOL form)
  • Production / development contract + any co-production agreements
  • Detailed budget separating IT / foreign costs (qualified expenditure)
  • Production schedule / plan (timeline, locations, key cast & crew)
  • Technical / artistic report for cultural eligibility (points test)
  • IP / rights documentation (author contracts, screenplay, treatment)
  • Company certificates and extracts
  • Proof of payment of the processing fee, between €200 and €10,000: without it the application is inadmissible (art. 2(3))
  • Distribution / sales letters of intent (if any)
  • For reserved quotas (young authors, docs, animation, cultural identity): specific supporting docs

3. Recommended operational steps

  1. Preliminary feasibility check, also with the studio Incentives Checker
  2. Prepare the detailed budget separating qualified expenditure incurred in Italy
  3. Prepare the cultural eligibility technical report (points table in the relevant DM)
  4. Submit the preliminary application + attachments to DGCOL, before significant expenditure begins
  5. Monitor platform communications and integration requests
  6. Retain all expenditure evidence (invoices, contracts, bank transfers) for the final application
  7. Submit the final application within deadlines after wrap / final accounting. The credit is used by way of set-off for 70% upon approval of the preliminary application and for the remainder upon approval of the final one; that share drops to 40% for undertakings with high production and financial capacity, and is 100% where only the final application is filed (art. 6(5))

4. Common pitfalls to avoid

  • Non-qualified or non-Italian-territory costs claimed as such
  • Incomplete or insufficient cultural eligibility documentation
  • Wrong timing (preliminary filed after work has started)
  • Budget not aligned with declared work type
  • Double counting expenditure across multiple support measures without clearance

5. Sources

  • Law no. 220 of 14 November 2016, art. 15
  • Interministerial Decree MiC-MEF of 10 July 2024, rep. no. 225, text of the decree
  • Interministerial Decree MiC-MEF of 22 April 2025, rep. no. 141, text of the amending decree, in force since 6 June 2025
  • Directorate General for Cinema and Audiovisual – national production tax credit
