Cookie Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

This page explains which cookies and similar tools the website studiolegalelofoco.com uses and how you can manage them. The controller is Avv. Andrea Lo Foco, as set out in the privacy notice.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. Some are needed to run and protect the site, others collect statistics on its use.

2. Technical cookies

They are needed for the site to work and stay secure and do not require consent.

  • cookiesAccepted, records that you have made a choice on the banner, so that it is not shown again. Duration 1 year.
  • cookiePreferences, stores your choice on statistics cookies, so that it can be respected and changed. Duration 1 year.
  • cf_clearance, set by Cloudflare, the service that delivers and protects the site, to tell visitors apart from automated traffic. Duration 1 year. If a check is needed, Cloudflare may set other technical security cookies, such as __cf_bm.

3. Google Analytics statistics cookies

They are set only if you consent. _ga and _ga_E7P6F0SVRY assign your browser a random identifier that lets Google Analytics recognise later visits and produce statistics on the use of the site. Their duration is set by Google and by default reaches 2 years from your last visit. Until you consent, the Google Analytics script is not loaded and no data are sent to Google for statistical purposes.

4. Third-party content loaded at your request

The Google Maps map in the contact section loads only if you click on its box. From then on Google receives your browser data and may set its own cookies, under its own privacy notice. The site's fonts are served with the pages and are not loaded from third-party services such as Google Fonts.

5. How consent works

On your first visit a banner offers three buttons. «Accept all» also enables statistics cookies, «Reject» keeps only the technical ones, «Manage cookies» lets you choose. Closing the banner with the X counts as a refusal. Your choice lasts one year.

6. How to change or withdraw consent

You can change your choice at any time from the «Cookie settings» button in the footer. If you withdraw consent, the Google Analytics cookies are deleted from your browser. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but blocking technical cookies may stop the site from working properly.
