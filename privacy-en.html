Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

This notice explains which personal data the website studiolegalelofoco.com processes, for what purposes, on which legal basis and for how long, under Article 13 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

2. Data we process

  • The data you send through the contact form, namely name, email address and the text of your message, with any information you choose to include.
  • The data you give us by email or telephone.
  • Technical browsing data, such as IP address, date and time of the request, page visited, browser and device type, processed by the services that deliver and protect the site (section 5).
  • Only if you consent, Google Analytics statistics, namely pages visited and interactions with the site, linked to a random identifier stored in a cookie in your browser, together with information on device, browser, referring page and approximate location derived from the IP address.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Answering your request and assessing a possible engagement. The legal basis is steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) or, for other kinds of request, our legitimate interest in replying (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Performing the engagement, if given, and complying with legal and professional obligations. The legal bases are performance of a contract and compliance with legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR).
  • Running the site and protecting it from abuse and cyber attacks. The legal basis is the firm's legitimate interest in the security and proper functioning of the site (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Measuring use of the site statistically with Google Analytics. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you may withdraw at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

Please do not include in the form data about health, criminal proceedings or other people unless it is needed to understand your request. If we receive such data, we will process it only as far as necessary to assess the engagement and to establish, exercise or defend legal claims (Art. 9(2)(f) and Art. 10 GDPR; Art. 2-octies(3)(e) of the Italian Privacy Code), and in any case within the limits allowed by law.

4. Whether you must provide the data

Providing the form data is optional, but without name, email and message we cannot reply. Technical browsing data are needed to show you the site. Consent to Google Analytics is optional and refusing it does not limit your use of the site in any way. We do not take decisions based solely on automated processing.

5. Recipients

The data are processed by the lawyer acting as controller and by the firm's staff and associates authorised to process data and bound by professional secrecy. They may also be processed, on the firm's behalf or as part of their own services, by these providers.

  • Formspree Inc. (United States), which receives messages sent through the form and forwards them to the firm by email. When you submit, your browser connects to Formspree's servers, which may display an anti-spam check (Google reCAPTCHA) under its own privacy notice.
  • Apple (iCloud Mail service), which handles the firm's email.
  • Cloudflare Inc. (United States), which delivers the site's pages and protects it from automated traffic and cyber attacks.
  • GitHub Inc. (United States), which hosts the site's files.
  • Google Ireland Limited, for Google Analytics, only if you consent, and for the Google Maps map, only if you choose to load it. For data collected through the map, Google acts under its own privacy notice. In the firm's Google Analytics account, Google Signals and the options for sharing data with Google are turned off.

Data are not sold or disclosed to third parties for marketing purposes.

6. Transfers outside the European Union

Formspree, Apple, Cloudflare, GitHub and Google may process data in the United States. Transfers take place with the safeguards under Chapter V GDPR set out by each provider in its own terms, such as the adequacy decision on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission. You can obtain a copy by writing to info@studiolegalelofoco.com.

7. How long we keep the data

  • Requests not followed by an engagement, up to 12 months from receipt or from the last communication, then deleted from the mailbox and from the form service.
  • Requests leading to an engagement, for the duration of the engagement and then for as long as required by legal and professional obligations.
  • Technical browsing data, for as long as strictly necessary for security, under the policies of the providers listed in section 5.
  • Google Analytics data linked to your browser's identifier, 14 months, a period that restarts with each new visit; detailed data on individual events, 2 months. When the period expires Google deletes them automatically. Only the aggregate statistics in the reports remain, which do not refer to individual visitors.
  • Cookies last as stated in the Cookie Policy.

8. Your rights

You may request access to your data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and portability, and you may object to processing based on legitimate interest. You can withdraw consent to Google Analytics at any time from the «Cookie settings» button in the footer. To exercise your rights write to info@studiolegalelofoco.com. We reply within one month of the request, a period that may be extended by two further months in the cases set out in Art. 12(3) GDPR. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, www.garanteprivacy.it).

9. Cookies

The site uses technical cookies and, only with your consent, Google Analytics cookies. The full list, with duration and purpose, is in the Cookie Policy.
