Resources & Analysis

tax credit

Tax Credit for Cinematographic and Audiovisual Production

Tax credit on eligible expenditure for works of Italian nationality, including recognised co-productions. Following the amendments of Interministerial Decree 281/2026, the credit is 30% for films by independent producers and 20%, which may be raised to 30% in the cases set out in art. 17, for TV and web works, up to €4m per film and €7m per TV or web work. As a rule, total public support may not exceed 50% of the cost of the work.

tax credit videogamestax-creditnationality

Video game production tax credit

Tax credit of 25% of eligible cost for Italian-nationality video games recognised as having cultural value, up to €1m a year per company or group (Interministerial Decree 12 May 2021 rep. 187). The credit and other public aid may not together exceed 50% of eligible cost, and an amount at least equal to the credit must be spent in the European Economic Area.

Read more
tax credit

Tax credit for attracting film and audiovisual investment to Italy (foreign productions)

The beneficiary is the Italian production services or post-production company working on commission, not the foreign client. A credit of 40% of the eligible cost (30% on above-the-line costs of individuals not resident in the European Economic Area), within the allocated budget and the ceiling of 50% of the cost of the work for total public support.

selective grants

Selective Contributions for Screenwriting, Development and Production

Annual calls under Art. 26 of Law 220/2016, with grants awarded on the basis of an expert Committee's assessment and approved by decree of the Director-General. Both windows of the 2026 call, 3 to 17 June and 1 to 15 October, are closed. In development and production the application also had to include, on pain of inadmissibility, natural-catastrophe insurance, and the author contracts listed in the rights declaration had to include the artificial intelligence opt-out clause.

How the Committee assesses projects, how long the rankings take, when the advance and the balance are paid, and when a grant lapses or is revoked. For the first 2026 window the 120-day deadline for the rankings expired on 15 October 2026, and as of 23 November they have not been published.

co-productions

International co-productions and bilateral funds

Together with its counterpart agencies, the DGCA has set up bilateral funds with France, Tunisia, Portugal and the Baltic States. The French fund supports the development of films and series and the co-production of films, the latter up to €200,000 and 50% of eligible costs, as a rule for Italian majority or equal-share projects and exceptionally also for Italian minority co-producers. Minority co-productions have a dedicated call, with €5m in 2026.

Cultural eligibility and nationality, requirements not to underestimate

For the production tax credit a work needs both Italian nationality and cultural eligibility. Cultural eligibility requires at least 50/100 under Table A of Interministerial Decree 225/2024, while nationality as a rule requires at least 70/100 under the tables of the Prime Ministerial Decree of 11 July 2017, which differ by type of work.

Cumulability of Tax Credit, selective contributions and regional incentives

Cumulation rules between national measures, bilateral agreements, Creative Europe MEDIA and regional film commissions. For the production tax credit the general constraint is the ceiling on total public support, as a rule 50% of the cost of the work, raised to 60, 80 or 100% in the cases of art. 4 of Decree 225/2024.

Tax credit, what is in force and what is changing (2026)

The rules in force are Interministerial Decree MiC-MEF of 10 July 2024 rep. 225, as amended by Decree of 22 April 2025 rep. 141 and by Decree of 6 July 2026 rep. 281, which lowers rates and per-work caps, introduces annual caps per company of €7m and €15m, lowers to €3m the cap for non-independent producers and contains no transitional provisions. The 2026 session, opened by Directorial Decree of 24 September 2026 rep. 2821, runs under the amended text. The film bill became Law no. 214 of 6 November 2026 (Official Journal no. 274 of 24 November 2026), in force from 9 December, which does not amend the tax credit rules.

Digital replicas of performers, voice, biometric data and the AI Act

A digital replica of a performer's voice is personal data and often biometric data, it is a deep fake to be disclosed under the AI Act and it touches related rights, yet none of these gives the artist a full remedy. The firmest protection remains personal identity, alongside Article 612-quater of the Italian Criminal Code introduced by Law 132/2025.
